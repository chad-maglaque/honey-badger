The day in 1,000 beats — sky colour from the Sun's altitude at this location
000204060810 gong

How it works. One day is 10 gongs, each gong is 100 beats, each beat is 100 ticks: 1,000 beats or 100,000 ticks a day. A beat is 86.4 seconds and a tick is 0.864 seconds. Noon is 05:00:00.

References. Civil local follows your time zone, including daylight saving: the display skips or repeats an hour on those days, like a wall clock. UTC is the same everywhere. Mean solar uses your longitude, ignoring political time zones. True solar adds the equation of time, so 05:00:00 is when the Sun is highest.

Ke-Shi borrows names from East Asian timekeeping (更 watches, 分, 刻). Its 10 × 100 × 100 arithmetic is modern, not an historical Chinese standard. Sky colours are artistic, not a weather report.
