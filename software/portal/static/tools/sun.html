Find direction without a compass

  • At solar noon (shown above) the sun is due south in the northern hemisphere, due north in the southern.
  • Northern hemisphere at night: Polaris sits almost exactly over true north, about as high above the horizon as your latitude.
  • Shadow-stick: mark a shadow tip, wait 15 min, mark again; the line from first to second mark points roughly east.
