Boiling is best. Bring clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 ft / 2,000 m). Let it cool covered. Chlorine bleach does not reliably kill Cryptosporidium; filter first if you can.

Stir, cover and wait 30 minutes. It should smell faintly of chlorine. If not, add the same dose again and wait another 15 minutes. If it still doesn’t smell of chlorine, use different water.

Use only

Plain, unscented household chlorine bleach. Not “splash-less”, color-safe, scented, or bleach with added cleaners. Check the label for the sodium hypochlorite %. Bleach loses strength over time; a bottle older than a year may be weak — use the upper end of the dose.
