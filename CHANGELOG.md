# Changelog

All notable changes to Honey Badger™. Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; the running version is in
[`VERSION`](VERSION) and on the launcher's Status page and the front panel.

## Unreleased

### Hardware
- **Slimmer case:** the four front-to-rear edge bumpers are gone (with their dovetail grooves in the side walls and
  the tenon slots in the frames). The front and rear bumper frames now protect the 8 corners and the front and rear
  edges, and the back edge of each frame's wrap is a full R5.4 round-over that blends the 6 mm step into the case.
- The case's own long edges are rounder: R4 along the bottom of the base (was R2) and R2.5 along the top of the lid
  (was R1.7). The frame bottom screws moved to new bosses just inside the rounded bottom edge.
- The two side lid screws are ordinary visible screws again (M3 × 6 in counterbores). Opening the case: frames off
  (6 screws each), 2 side screws out, lift the lid.
- Taller exhaust louvers: four 5 × 30 mm slots (were 5 × 20), high on the right wall.
- Lid artwork re-centred; TPU for one Badger back to about 150 g.

## v0.4 — 2026-11-17 (pre-release)

The first tagged release. Everything is built and tested in software (unit, adversarial, red-team and
end-to-end tests in a virtual machine); **it has not yet run on real Raspberry Pi hardware** — see
"Needs real hardware" below.

### The Badger
- **Offline knowledge server** on a Raspberry Pi 5: library (Kiwix and more), "Ask the library" AI with
  citations, offline maps, tools, files, a launcher for phones on its own Wi-Fi hotspot, front panel
  (OLED + knob), updater with signed software updates, USB bundles and peers, builder for flashing.
- **Problem solver** — science, engineering, game theory, basic coding and survival: deep-solve jobs with
  live steps; exact calculators (units, electrical/solar, beams/ropes, pipes/pumps, heat, water treatment,
  radio, game theory, linear programming, navigation, weather); library citations; a sandboxed Python
  runner (isolated per run, no network, no family data); AI model roles (fast, reasoning, coder) with a
  self-test, promotion and rollback; models update like any other content.
- **Feature plugins**: NOAA forecast as text, voice mode, aircraft (ADS-B), weather satellites (Meteor-M),
  family document vault, supplies & rationing, neighbourhood aid-station log, skills & resources
  directory, missing persons / lost & found, sensor station.
- **Mesh (Meshtastic)**: community and family boards bridged to the radio; radio commands with per-command
  levels; NOAA alert relays; trusted circles; Badger-to-Badger courtesy for a mesh of many Badgers (one
  relay/reply per channel, priorities, channel-busy deferral, budgets scaled by peer count, boot quiet
  period, echo guards); stranger reply budgets; no device name over the air; sealed, padded control
  packets on private channels only; radio pairing confirmed from the radio.
- **Content licensing**: every catalog item marked any/personal use; units built for sale never preload
  personal-use content — the owner accepts and downloads it at hand-off.

### Security (prime directive: the Badger must never be turned against its owners)
- **Visitors** can't locate the house (fixed ~10 km grid for every visitor-facing position) or learn who's
  home; visitor-safe status; USB files and sensors members-only by default; visitors' posts reach the
  radio only with member approval.
- **Wi-Fi**: neutral default name, random WPA2 password by default, Enhanced Open (OWE) and an
  experimental OWE-transition mode, client isolation; the vault refuses plain HTTP on an open hotspot.
- **Insiders**: devices for existing members need that member's (or the panel's) approval, announced on
  the family board; family key shown to members and the panel only; passphrase/scrypt admin secret;
  optional front-panel lock with recovery; key rotation on member removal; aid volunteers see only their
  own records.
- **Sealed records**: supplies, aid log, family board history, member details and contact details are
  encrypted at rest; new entries are saved while locked; reading needs the owner passphrase; printable
  recovery key; family radio messages from the last 48 h stay readable to members while locked.
- **Security posture**: Neighbourly / Guarded (default) / Lockdown — Lockdown also mutes the radio
  (CLIENT_MUTE, no name/position/telemetry broadcasts) and force-locks sealed records; radio state kept
  in RAM only.
- **Panic wipe** (crypto-erase: keys first, then data; new radio keys; the box is left as a plain
  library), **handover/resale reset**, optional **dead-man switch**.
- **Duress**: a duress PIN, duress passphrase and panel duress code give a normal-looking reply and show a
  plausible made-up household (generated by default on every Badger); by default duress **locks without
  wiping** (recover everything with the recovery card); wiping is opt-in.
- **"What this Badger reveals"**: a live checklist of anything the Badger is giving away, with one-tap fixes.
- Supply-chain hardening: add-on apps on their own origin, pinned upstream code, peer trust scoped by
  kind, name-constrained local CA with its key destroyed after use, encrypted device signing key, logs
  off or in RAM, no USB boot.

### Robustness (end-to-end pressure test in a VM)
Static llama.cpp build, auto-restart for nginx/dnsmasq/chrony/gpsd, honest "no radio" status, SatDump on
Debian 13, portal backlog and thread cap, clean 413/429/507 replies, per-IP connection limits, Kolibri
bound to localhost and a tight hotspot firewall, monotonic timers (clock jumps are harmless), private
file modes, quiet logs.

### Hardware
- 3D-printed enclosure (PETG + TPU): **front and rear bumper frames plus four full-size edge bumpers**
  (dovetail tongues, end tenons captured by the frames) covering every outside edge; four rain-shedding
  vents; SDR 2 shelf and rear SDR 2 jack (aircraft and weather satellites standard); M8 SENSORS socket.
- Full BOM down to every screw and small electronic part, a one-page print sheet with a kit checklist,
  renders.

### Docs
Quick start, setup and usage guides; features overview; operations, security, mesh and hardware guides;
a docs test that fails when the guides fall behind the product.

### Needs real hardware
Radios (Meshtastic firmware behaviour, PRIVATE_APP delivery, CLIENT_MUTE, position precision), both
RTL-SDRs (alerts, readsb, SatDump, serials), GPS, sensors, OLED/knob, OWE on the Wi-Fi adapter, UPS and
power-cut behaviour, Pi 5 timing (AI speed, sealed-record unlock, scrypt), the sandbox on the Pi, model
checksums and the piper text-to-speech checksum (not published upstream).
